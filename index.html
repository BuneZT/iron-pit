CHRONICLE OF THE PIT

I. THE DESECRATED SLEEP

Seeking refuge from a killing ash storm, a lone traveler struck a fire upon an ancient slab of black stone. It was no hearth, but the binding seal of Mor'gath, the Ash Sovereign. The flame broke the seal, waking the demon from centuries of slumber.

II. THE SOVEREIGN'S TETHER

Enraged by mortal insolence, Mor'gath dragged the intruder into the sunken Iron Pit, cursing them to die and resurrect in an endless cycle. Yet awoken prematurely, the demon cannot walk the earth; each fiend it spawns is a living sliver of its own soul. Every fiend slain permanently starves the demon.

III. THE TENTH BELL

In supreme arrogance, Mor'gath swore a dark blood-oath: no mortal could survive ten onslaughts. If ten bells toll and the traveler still draws breath, the demon's tether shatters, the re-sanctified altar ignites, and the pit shall grant passage to the light.
